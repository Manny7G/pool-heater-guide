Welcome!

We hope you enjoy your pool and spa experience. Your pool uses a heat pump system that warms the water gradually.

Important: Heating may take approximately 3–4 hours depending on weather conditions.

🔥 Turning ON / OFF the Heater

Step 1: Select Heat / Cool

From the main screen, tap:

HEAT / COOL

Step 2: Select What You Want to Heat

Choose whether you want to heat:

  • 🏊 Pool
  • ♨️ Spa

Step 3: Set Your Temperature

On the top left side of the screen, swipe/drag the switch to the right. Then select your desired temperature.

Step 4: Confirm Your Selection

After selecting the temperature, tap the check mark ✔️ on the bottom right of the screen.

♨️ Using Only the Spa / Hot Tub

If you only want to use the spa, follow these steps:

  1. Turn ON the pump.
  2. Turn ON the heater.
  3. Press the arrow to change the setting to SPA.
  4. Select your desired temperature.

💧 Turning ON the Filter Pump

Step 1

Tap:

FILTER PUMP

Step 2

On the top left side of the screen, swipe the switch to the right until it says:

ON

⚠️ Important: Wait about 15 seconds so the switches in the back can turn on the pump. The spa handles should be facing outward.

💨 Turning ON the Blower / Jets

Step 1: Open Features

From the main page, select:

FEATURES

Step 2: Choose Location

Select whether you want the blower for:

  • ♨️ Spa
  • 🏊 Full Pool

Step 3: Select Blower

Step 4: Turn Blower ON

On the left side of the screen, slide the switch to the right to turn it ON.

💡 Pool Lights

Customize the pool and spa lighting with different colors and lighting effects.

Step 1: Open the Lights Menu

From the main screen, tap:

LIGHTS

Step 2: Select "All Bodies"

Tap All Bodies to control the lights for the entire pool and spa.

Step 3: Turn the Lights ON

On the top left of the screen, swipe the switch to the right until it shows ON.

Step 4: Choose a Lighting Effect

Select your preferred lighting mode, such as:

  • 🌈 Color Change
  • ✨ Twinkle
  • 💙 Solid Color
  • 🌊 Party Mode
  • 🎨 Any other available lighting effect

💡 Tip: Feel free to choose the lighting effect you like best to enhance your evening by the pool.

⚠️ Important Notes for Your Stay

  • The heater may take approximately 3–4 hours to warm the pool and spa.
  • The pool operates on a timer and may enter power-saving mode around 6:00 PM.
  • To remove power-saving mode:
    • Go to the Filter Pump icon.
    • Select HIGH.
  • Please turn OFF the heater after you finish using the pool and spa.

💧 Refilling the Pool After Heating

When using the heater, please refill the pool water if needed.

  1. Locate the hose next to the pool screen.
  2. Pull the hose out of the gray cover.
  3. Place the hose over the edge of the pool.
  4. Turn the valve ON to add water.
  5. Turn the valve OFF once the pool is filled.
