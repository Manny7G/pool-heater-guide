Pool Heater Types

Gas Heaters: Fast heating and great for quickly warming pools and spas.

Gas heaters use natural gas or propane and are ideal when you need rapid temperature changes.

Heat Pumps: Energy-efficient heating using outside air.

Heat pumps are excellent for maintaining steady temperatures while using less energy.

Solar Heating: Uses sunlight to warm pool water.

Solar systems have low operating costs but depend on sunlight availability.

Common Problems

  • Heater will not turn on
  • Pool temperature is too low
  • Error codes appearing
  • Low water flow problems
  • Pump or filter issues

Maintenance Checklist

  • Check filters regularly
  • Keep water chemistry balanced
  • Inspect heater connections
  • Remove debris around equipment
  • Schedule professional service when needed

Energy Saving Tips

  • Use a pool cover when not swimming
  • Maintain proper water chemistry
  • Run heating during the most efficient hours
  • Keep equipment clean

How to Use the Pool Heater

Your pool uses an energy-efficient heat pump. Heat pumps warm the pool gradually and work best when the pool pump is running.

  1. Make sure the pool pump is turned ON.
  2. Turn the pool heater ON using the control panel.
  3. Set your desired temperature.
  4. Allow time for the pool to warm up.

Please be patient — heat pumps may require several hours to increase the pool temperature, especially during cooler weather.

Recommended Temperatures

  • Pool: 78°F - 82°F
  • Spa: 100°F - 104°F

For energy efficiency, please lower the temperature when the pool is not being used.

Important Guest Guidelines

  • Please do not adjust valves or plumbing controls.
  • Please do not open equipment panels.
  • Please keep the pool equipment area clear.
  • Please do not turn off the pool pump while heating.

If the Pool Is Not Heating

  • Confirm the pool pump is running.
  • Check that the heater is turned ON.
  • Make sure the set temperature is higher than the current pool temperature.
  • Allow additional time for heating.
