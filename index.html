Welcome

This guide helps pool owners understand their heating system, identify common issues, and keep their pool comfortable and efficient.

Pool Heater Types

Gas Heaters: Fast heating and great for quickly warming pools and spas.

Gas heaters use natural gas or propane and are ideal when you need rapid temperature changes.

Heat Pumps: Energy-efficient heating using outside air.

Heat pumps are excellent for maintaining steady temperatures while using less energy.

Solar Heating: Uses sunlight to warm pool water.

Solar systems have low operating costs but depend on sunlight availability.

Common Problems

  • Heater will not turn on
  • Pool temperature is too low
  • Error codes appearing
  • Low water flow problems
  • Pump or filter issues

Maintenance Checklist

  • Check filters regularly
  • Keep water chemistry balanced
  • Inspect heater connections
  • Remove debris around equipment
  • Schedule professional service when needed

Energy Saving Tips

  • Use a pool cover when not swimming
  • Maintain proper water chemistry
  • Run heating during the most efficient hours
  • Keep equipment clean
