Welcome!

Thank you for choosing Manny G Properties.

We've created this digital guide to make using the pool and spa easy for everyone. Simply follow the steps below.

📱 Tip: You can always return to the menu above to jump directly to any section.

🔥 Turn the Heater ON

Follow these steps to heat either the pool or the spa.

STEP 1

Tap Heat / Cool

From the main screen, tap the Heat / Cool button.

STEP 2

Select What You Want to Heat

Choose whether you want to heat the:

  • 🏊 Pool
  • ♨️ Spa
STEP 3

Select Your Temperature

Move the switch at the top left to the ON position, then choose your desired temperature.

STEP 4

Save Your Selection

Tap the ✔ check mark in the lower-right corner of the screen to confirm.

♨️ Using the Hot Tub / Spa Only

If you only want to enjoy the spa, follow these steps.

STEP 1

Turn ON the Filter Pump

Before heating the spa, make sure the filter pump is turned ON.

STEP 2

Select SPA Mode

Turn the heater ON, then press the arrow to change from Pool to Spa.

STEP 3

Choose the Temperature

Select your desired spa temperature and tap the ✔ check mark to save.

💡 Helpful Tip

The spa heats much faster than the full pool and is the best option if you want warm water quickly.

💧 Turn ON the Filter Pump

The filter pump must be ON before heating the pool or spa.

STEP 1

Select Filter Pump

From the main screen, tap the Filter Pump icon.

STEP 2

Turn the Pump ON

Slide the switch located in the upper-left corner to the ON position.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

Wait approximately 15 seconds for the valves behind the equipment to move into position.

Once the valves finish moving, the handles should be facing outward.

💨 Turn ON the Blower / Jets

Enjoy relaxing spa bubbles by turning on the blower.

STEP 1

Open Features

From the main screen, tap Features.

STEP 2

Select the Area

Choose whether you would like the blower for the Spa or the Entire Pool.

STEP 3

Select Blower

Tap Blower.

STEP 4

Turn the Blower ON

Slide the switch to the ON position.

✨ Guest Tip

The blower creates relaxing bubbles and is perfect for enjoying the spa in the evening.

💡 Pool Lights

Create the perfect evening atmosphere by customizing the pool and spa lights.

STEP 1

Open the Lights Menu

From the main screen, tap Lights.

STEP 2

Select "All Bodies"

Tap All Bodies to control the pool and spa lights together.

STEP 3

Turn the Lights ON

Slide the switch in the upper-left corner to the ON position.

STEP 4

Select Your Favorite Lighting Effect

Choose the lighting mode you would like to enjoy, such as:

  • 🌈 Color Change
  • 💙 Solid Color
  • ✨ Twinkle
  • 🎉 Party Mode
  • 🎨 Any other available lighting effect

🌙 Evening Tip

The lights create a beautiful atmosphere after sunset and are perfect for relaxing by the pool or spa.

⚠️ Important Notes for Your Stay

🔥 The pool heater may take approximately 3–4 hours to heat the water depending on the outside temperature.

⚡ The pool system enters Power Saving Mode around 6:00 PM.

💧 To exit Power Saving Mode, open Filter Pump and select HIGH.

♨️ Please turn the heater OFF when you are finished using the pool or spa.

💧 Refilling the Pool After Heating

Please refill the pool after using the heater.

  1. Locate the hose beside the pool equipment.
  2. Pull the hose from the gray cover.
  3. Place the hose over the edge of the pool.
  4. Turn the water valve ON.
  5. Turn the valve OFF once the pool has been refilled.
