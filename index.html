🏊 Pool & Spa Guide

Welcome! Follow the instructions below to safely operate the pool and spa.

🔥 Heat the Pool

Turn on the pool heater and set your desired temperature.

  1. Tap Heat / Cool.
  2. Select the area you want to heat.
  3. Slide the switch to ON.
  4. Select your desired temperature.
  5. Tap the ✔ checkmark to save.
Next: Spa Mode

♨️ Spa Only

  1. Turn on the Filter Pump.
  2. Select Spa.
  3. Choose your temperature.
  4. Wait approximately 15 seconds for the valves to switch.
  5. Turn the heater ON.
Next: Blower

💨 Blower / Jets

  1. Select Features.
  2. Select Pool or Spa.
  3. Tap Blower.
  4. Slide the switch to ON.
Next: Notes

⚠ Important Notes

  • Heating the pool can take approximately 3–4 hours.
  • The system enters Power Saving Mode around 6:00 PM.
  • Set the Pool Filter Pump to HIGH to exit Power Saving Mode.
  • Please turn the heater OFF after using the pool or spa.
  • Refill the pool after using the heater as instructed.
